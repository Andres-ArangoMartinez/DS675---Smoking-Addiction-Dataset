# Milestone 4 - Do Your Own Experiments

## Task Description

### Objectives

For this final stage of the Mini-Project, you will work with your group on the dataset you agreed on in Milestone 3.

There are no specific requirements or restrictions on the topic for Milestone 4, except that it must involve a supervised Machine Learning prediction task. In other respects, you can apply your own curiosity and creativity. For example, you could:

- Modify previous work in order to obtain higher accuracy or other measures of performance.
- Create new learning models that others had not considered for your chosen dataset.
- Work on complementary questions that have not been considered in other people's work.

## Mini-Project Deliverables

- The main deliverable is a 5-8 page report on the ideas you tried, and the practical results you were able to obtain. In your report, you should emphasize what differentiates your own work from previous works on Kaggle. The report should contain enough information in order to judge the correctness and appropriateness of your design choices, with respect to datasets and their preparation, learning models and their implementation (whether implemented by you or by Kaggle authors), and the evaluation and comparison of model performance.
- In addition to the report, you will create a 5-10 minute video presentation of your work.
- The contributions and responsibilities of each member must be clearly stated in the report, and all members are expected to contribute to the report and to participate in the video presentation.
- By default, every member of the group would be given the same grade for Milestone 4. However, these grades may be adjusted upwards or downwards depending on the degree to which the student has been seen to contribute, and the quality of the contributions.
- For maximum credit, your report and video presentation should be well-organized, neat, relevant and thorough. For more details on the grading standards, please see the rubric at the bottom of this page.

## Submitting Your Work

1. Your report must be in .pdf or .ipynb format, and should link to the proposal for your chosen dataset that was submitted for Milestone 2.
2. Your report should also link to your code and the video, so that all the necessary information can be submitted together in a single uploaded file.
3. The project deliverable must be a single report and a single video for the group, with no exceptions. Note that any new submission by a member of the group will overwrite all previous submissions made by any of the group members. The submission must list the names of all participating group members.
4. Before the Milestone 4 deadline (middle of Week 14), you must also submit your report and video together with a brief description to the Week 14 Discussion Forum. Your project outcomes will be the "resources" to be discussed in that week.

## Notes

- Your Mini-Project reports and videos will be the topic for class discussion in Week 14. Please be sure to submit what you have by the deadline.
- Groups are strongly encouraged to make appointments with the lecturer for feedback on their plans and progress, as soon as possible after Milestone 3.
- Submission of early drafts of your report is also strongly encouraged. This allows us to track the progress of your group towards meeting its objectives, and to give you better guidance. Keep in mind that you can submit as many times as you like up until the close of submissions.
