\section{Introduction}
The proposed supervised regression task is to predict
\texttt{Overall\_Craving\_Score} and compare the contribution of psychological
measurements, daily habits, and historical craving and nicotine-use information.
This direction builds on the original Milestone 2 proposal~\cite{proposal}.
Next-day prediction for known participants is a candidate scenario; the team has
not finalized the horizon, evaluation population, feature groups, or protocol.
