# Related-work source collection

This folder brings together the project's related-work source collection, a written background synthesis, and a reading library. Start with [Background.md](Background.md) for the comparison of prior work and its relevance to the selected dataset; use the tables below to locate original publications, datasets, and code. This is an expandable narrative review, not a completed systematic review. Inclusion of a source does not establish adoption of its methods or reproduction of its results.

**Initial research:** November 10, 2026. **Initial collection and selected citation metadata checks:** November 11, 2026. **Library verification record:** November 15, 2026. **README reviewed against the current background and local evidence:** November 18, 2026. The README revision does not represent a new literature search.

**Project context:** [Project overview](../../../README.md) · [Current methodology and Kate's proposed extension](../../../docs/methodology.md) · [Dataset access and verified file](../../../data/README.md) · [Team decision log](../../../docs/decisions.md).

The selected resource is described as **synthetic daily nicotine-pouch data**, not a clinical cohort of cigarette smokers. The downloaded [Kaggle version 1](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/datascikhan/the-nicotine-craving-and-anxiety-dynamics-dataset/versions/1) has **30,000 records, 35 columns, and 1,000 simulated participants with days 1–30 each**. The local audit records no duplicate participant-day keys and 349 missing `Overall_Craving_Score` values. Its 4,286,125-byte CSV has SHA-256 `7218b9c5925d3c4b69ef7dc0153861778f8a9e4deb67dfa5a7242c7a0d13b907`, rechecked for this revision. [Data documentation](../../../data/README.md) and the versioned [provenance verifier](../../../data/provenance_AI_assisted/README.md) document the selected version, direct CSV comparisons, and the CC0: Public Domain license reported by publisher metadata. File identity and dimensions are established; the generation mechanism, score construction, and correspondence to validated human measurements remain unresolved.

**Reading guide:** Results below are reported by their sources, not reproduced by our team. “Requires verification” identifies missing evidence; “proposed” identifies plans rather than completed analyses. Momentary and daily ratings, cigarettes and nicotine pouches, and current-state classification and future prediction are not interchangeable. Metrics from different outcomes and evaluation designs should not be ranked against one another.

## How Background.md organizes the evidence

[Section 1](Background.md#1-overview-of-previous-work) identifies each work's objective, methods, task, and scope. [Section 2](Background.md#2-about-previous-work) relates that evidence to the dataset through four axes:

| --- | --- | --- |
| [2.1 Psychological and behavioral factors](Background.md#21-psychological-and-behavioral-factors-associated-with-nicotine-craving-and-use) | Anxiety, stress, mood, sleep, craving, and nicotine-use fields are read alongside Spillane, McKinty, Freeman, Reed, and Chapman. | A shared construct or column label does not establish the same instrument, causal effect, or empirical relationship in synthetic records. Freeman concerns broader substance use; Chapman provides short-term pouch-product context. |
| [2.2 Tasks, models, and temporal horizons](Background.md#22-predictive-tasks-models-and-temporal-horizons) | Fisher's later smoking classification, mCrave's minute-level estimation, Perski's lapse tasks, Leppin's next-assessment outcomes, Hébert's time to first lapse, and Lee's next-day consumption are contrasted with Nugroho's daily-score models. | Same-assessment classification, current-state estimation, future prediction, and survival analysis answer different questions. Daily columns are not timestamped EMA streams. |
| [2.3 Performance and methodological limitations](Background.md#23-predictive-performance-and-methodological-limitations) | Participant/day dependence, input timing, temporal versus participant holdouts, and incremental feature value guide interpretation of reported scores. | Nugroho's contemporaneous fitted models and separate lag-based persistence baseline are not a matched next-day comparison. Kapoor and Davis explain why leakage must be assessed against intended use and information availability. |
| [2.4 Data sources and generalizability](Background.md#24-data-sources-and-generalizability-of-findings) | Human smoking studies, the shared EMASENS cohort, a study protocol, a pouch trial, and synthetic-data methodology are placed in context. | Leppin reuses EMASENS; Ra reports a protocol rather than findings; Adams does not validate this Kaggle generator. Clinical findings and published scores cannot be assumed to transfer to the simulation. |

The current synthesis supports the relevance of the recorded concepts and makes task differences explicit. It does not establish a new contribution or clinical validity. Kate's feature-group comparison remains an existing proposal; its added predictive value requires a documented comparison and measured results.

## 1. Work using our exact dataset

**The direct computational reference and CSV identity have been identified.** The earlier local review identifies Nugroho's original Kaggle notebook as version 1 and compares its cell sources with the GitHub mirror. Dataset identity is now reproducibly documented by the versioned [provenance verifier and source record](../../../data/provenance_AI_assisted/README.md): the recorded November 18 comparison confirms that the Kaggle v1 CSV and pinned NumiKun CSV have identical bytes. The CSV verifier does not reproduce the notebook's saved model results or perform its cell-source comparison. No scientific paper using this exact file was verified in the documented searches; this does not establish that none exists.

| [Time-Series Forecasting, Kaggle version 1](https://www.kaggle.com/code/rizkisuryanugroho/time-series-forecasting?scriptVersionId=351856888) — Rizki Surya Nugroho (2026). [Pinned GitHub copy](https://github.com/NumiKun/Smoking-Addiction/blob/aa3faf512362f941721a85793e65baf5c3c7d760/Model/time_series_forecasting.ipynb). | **Version and dataset identity verified locally.** Of 58 cell sources, 57 are identical; cell 4 differs only in `DATA_PATH`. **Methodological reading:** [Background §2.3](Background.md#23-predictive-performance-and-methodological-limitations) documents two additional issues in the reference: missing persistence lags are replaced with a mean from held-out targets, and a later `TimeSeriesSplit` follows participant-first/day-second sorting rather than one global chronological order. These affect interpretation of the relevant analyses; they do not make every contemporaneous measurement invalid for a current-state estimation task.

**Follow-up from the existing proposal:** The [project methodology](../../../docs/methodology.md#comparison-with-kaggle-work) also lists *Time-Series Forecasting: Nicotine Craving and Anxiety Dynamics* and *Smoking Addiction Insights*. The forecasting entry is now identified as Nugroho's versioned notebook and its GitHub mirror, rather than two independent studies. The second notebook is not the selected comparison baseline; verify its exact version and role if it is used later.
